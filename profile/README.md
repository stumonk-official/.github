# Stumonk

## About Stumonk

Stumonk is a modern academic networking platform that brings **students, faculty, and universities together in one connected ecosystem**.

Our platform is designed to make academic communication, mentorship, collaboration, and campus engagement simpler and more accessible.

Stumonk helps institutions build stronger digital campus communities while giving students and faculty better ways to discover, connect, communicate, and collaborate.

## What Stumonk Does

### 🎓 For Students

- Discover faculty and researchers based on academic interests
- Connect directly with faculty and mentors
- Find academic opportunities, announcements, and events
- Participate in course and department communities
- Build meaningful academic relationships beyond the classroom

### 👨‍🏫 For Faculty

- Connect with students more effectively
- Make mentorship availability and expertise discoverable
- Share research, project, internship, and academic opportunities
- Manage academic conversations and interactions
- Build and participate in course and research communities

### 🏛️ For Universities

- Provide a centralized student-faculty communication platform
- Build institution-wide academic communities
- Improve access to mentorship and academic support
- Manage departments, programs, and campus communities
- Create a modern digital campus experience

## Platform Features

- **Student–Faculty Direct Connect** — Discover and connect with the right faculty.
- **Academic Communication** — Centralize conversations, announcements, and mentorship interactions.
- **Mentorship & Guidance** — Help students connect with faculty based on interests and expertise.
- **Academic Communities** — Create structured spaces for courses, departments, and research groups.
- **Profile Discovery** — Discover students, faculty, skills, research interests, and academic expertise.
- **Announcements & Opportunities** — Share important academic updates, events, internships, and opportunities.
- **Events & Sessions** — Coordinate academic sessions, seminars, office hours, and campus events.
- **Institution-Led Access** — Support role-based access and institution-managed academic communities.

## Our Mission

We believe meaningful academic connections should extend beyond the classroom.

Stumonk is building the infrastructure for connected academic communities where students can find guidance, faculty can extend their impact, and institutions can create stronger campus networks.

## How It Works

1. **Institution Onboarding** — Configure the institution, departments, roles, and access policies.
2. **Profile Setup** — Students and faculty create academic profiles with their interests, expertise, and associations.
3. **Connect & Collaborate** — Discover faculty, request mentorship, join communities, and communicate.
4. **Build a Smarter Campus** — Grow stronger academic networks across the institution.
